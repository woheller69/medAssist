medAssist - on device medical assistant

Discuss medical topics with your personal AI assistant.
The assistant takes your existing conditions, vitals, and medication into account for context,
but does not replace professional medical judgment.

- 100% Offline: No internet required.
- Privacy-First: No data ever leaves your phone.
- Powered by MedGemma: Based on Google’s open-weight medical LLM.


CRITICAL DISCLAIMER: READ BEFORE USING
medAssist is NOT a medical device and does NOT provide medical advice.
This application is an educational and informational tool only.
It uses a large language model (MedGemma) that can produce incorrect, outdated, or harmful information.
It has no understanding of your personal medical history beyond what you type, cannot perform clinical judgment,
and is not evaluated by any medical regulatory body.

NEVER rely on this app for diagnosis, treatment decisions, or emergency care.
Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
In case of emergency, call your local emergency services immediately.
